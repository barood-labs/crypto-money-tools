Eight ways to make the plan cheaper

  1. Shop your pantry and freezer first, then plan meals around what's there.
  2. Check the store's weekly ad before planning, not after.
  3. Plan two or three meals that share the same protein.
  4. Cook rice, beans and grains in big batches.
  5. Have one leftovers night a week.
  6. Buy frozen vegetables; they're cheap and don't spoil.
  7. Shop with a list and a set budget, and avoid shopping hungry.
  8. Track what you actually spend for a month to find the leaks.

FAQ

How much should a family of 4 spend on groceries per week?

It depends heavily on location and diet. Track your current spending for a month, then use the calculator to set a per-person, per-meal target a little lower.

What are the cheapest meals to make?

Meals built on rice, pasta, beans, lentils, eggs, potatoes, oats and frozen vegetables are usually the cheapest per serving.
