100 Envelope Challenge: Free Tracker and Chart

Number 100 envelopes from 1 to 100. Each day (or a few times a week), pick a random envelope and put that many dollars in it. Fill all 100 and you've saved $5,050. Doing one a day takes 100 days; two a week takes about a year.

Click an envelope below when you fill it. Your progress is saved in this browser only.

Cash or digital?

The original "cash stuffing" version uses real envelopes in a box or binder, which makes saving feel physical. If you don't keep cash at home, do it digitally: color the box here, then transfer that amount to a savings account. The math is the same.

Tips

  • Pull the big numbers on payday and small ones the week before.
  • Keep the cash somewhere safe, or deposit it every week.
  • Pair it with a sinking fund plan so you know what the money is for.

FAQ

How much is the 100 envelope challenge?

Filling envelopes 1 through 100 adds up to $5,050.

How long does the 100 envelope challenge take?

100 days at one envelope a day, or about a year at two envelopes a week.
