Gift Budget Per Person Calculator (Split Your Total Fairly)

Most gift budgets go wrong in the opposite direction from the Christmas budget: you know the total, but not how much to spend on each person. This calculator splits a fixed gift budget across everyone on your list using simple weights. A weight of 2 gets twice as much as a weight of 1.

How to make the list fit the budget

  • Cut people, not quality. Suggest a family Secret Santa or white elephant: one good gift each instead of eight small ones.
  • Group gifts. One $60 gift "to the Smiths" beats four $15 gifts.
  • Leave a 10% buffer. Forgotten people and last-minute invites always show up. Add a blank row with weight 0.5.
  • Track as you buy. Write the real price next to each name. Our free Holiday Budget Starter printable has a gift list tracker for this.

FAQ

How much should I spend per person on Christmas gifts?

Start with the total you can afford, then split it. Many families give a partner and kids the most, parents and siblings a middle amount, and $10 to $25 for friends, coworkers and teachers.

How do I split a gift budget fairly?

Give each person or group a weight (for example 3 for a partner, 2 for kids, 1 for siblings) and divide the total by the sum of the weights. The calculator does this for you and rounds the amounts.

What if my list is bigger than my budget?

Lower the weights for the outer circle, suggest a Secret Santa, or give group or homemade gifts. It is easier to trim the list before shopping than at the register.
