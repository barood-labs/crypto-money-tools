Crypto Exchange Fees Compared

Fees eat into DCA returns more than most people realize. Here's how the big exchanges charge.

ExchangeSimple buyPro/advanced tradingNotes
CoinbaseSpread plus a flat fee on the simple appMaker/taker tiers on AdvancedUS-friendly, easiest onboarding
KrakenHigher fee on Instant BuyLow maker/taker on Kraken ProStrong security record
BybitCard buys cost moreLow spot and derivatives feesNot available in the US

How to pay less

  • Use the "pro" or "advanced" screen instead of the one-tap buy.
  • Fund with a bank transfer, not a card.
  • Buy bigger amounts less often if there's a flat fee.
  • Log every fee, because fees add to your cost basis (see how).
