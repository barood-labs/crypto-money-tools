Hot vs Cold Crypto Wallets

Where you keep your crypto decides who controls it. There are three main options.

1. Exchange account (custodial)

The exchange holds the keys. It's easy and you can recover the account, but if the exchange freezes withdrawals or fails, your coins are at risk.

2. Hot wallet (software)

Apps like Phantom (Solana) or MetaMask (Ethereum) keep the keys on your phone or browser. They're free and fast, but they're only as safe as your device.

3. Cold wallet (hardware)

Devices like Ledger or Trezor keep the keys offline and sign transactions on the device. They cost about $60–$250 and are the standard choice for long-term holdings.

Quick rule

AmountSuggested setup
Under $500Hot wallet or exchange
$500–$5,000Hot wallet plus a backed-up seed phrase
Over $5,000Hardware wallet

Seed phrase safety

  • Write it on paper or metal, not in a screenshot.
  • Never type it into a website or give it to "support".
  • Store a copy in a second location.
