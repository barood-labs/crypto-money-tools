Why two discounts don't add up

"40% off plus an extra 10%" is not 50% off. The second discount applies to the already-reduced price: $100 becomes $60, then 10% off $60 is $54, so the real discount is 46%. The calculator shows the true combined percentage.

How to tell if a Black Friday deal is real

  • Check the price history. Price-tracking sites and browser extensions show what an item sold for over the past months. If the "deal" matches the usual price, skip it.
  • Compare the exact model number. Some Black Friday TVs and appliances are special cheaper models made just for the sale.
  • Add shipping and tax. A lower price with $10 shipping can lose to a slightly higher price with free shipping. Use the comparison tool above.
  • Only buy what was on your list. Write a list with a target price before the sales start. Something you didn't need isn't a deal at any discount.

FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and subtract it. For example, 30% off $80: $80 x 0.30 = $24 off, so you pay $56.

Is 20% off plus an extra 20% off the same as 40% off?

No. The second 20% comes off the already-discounted price, so the total is 36% off. 100 becomes 80, and 20% off 80 is 64.

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27, the day after US Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26). Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026.
