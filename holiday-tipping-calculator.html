Rules worth knowing

  • Mail carriers: USPS employees may accept a gift worth up to $20 per occasion, but not cash or cash equivalents such as checks or prepaid cards that work like cash. Cookies or a small store gift card are fine.
  • Teachers and public employees: many schools and agencies cap gift values; a class group gift avoids the problem.
  • Short on cash? A handwritten card or homemade treat is a real thank-you. Tipping should fit your budget, not break it.
  • Timing: give in mid to late December, before people leave for their own holidays.

FAQ

How much should I tip my house cleaner for the holidays?

A common guideline is the cost of one regular cleaning, given in December. Adjust up or down based on how often they come and your budget.

Can I give my mail carrier a cash tip?

No. USPS rules let carriers accept gifts worth up to $20 per occasion, but not cash or cash equivalents. A small gift, snacks or a non-cash gift card is fine.

Do I have to tip everyone at the holidays?

No. Holiday tips are a thank-you, not an obligation. Focus on people who help you regularly, and a card or homemade treat is a fine alternative if money is tight.
