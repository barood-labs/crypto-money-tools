How pivot points are calculated

The Classic pivot is P = (high + low + close) ÷ 3. Then R1 = 2P − low, S1 = 2P − high, R2 = P + (high − low), S2 = P − (high − low), R3 = high + 2(P − low) and S3 = low − 2(high − P). Fibonacci pivots use the same P but add or subtract 38.2%, 61.8% and 100% of the range. Woodie pivots weight the close twice: P = (high + low + 2 × close) ÷ 4. Camarilla levels sit close to the last close: close ± range × 1.1 ÷ 12, ÷ 6 and ÷ 4 for levels 1 to 3.

Worked example

Yesterday BTC traded between $68,000 and $72,000 and closed at $71,000. The classic pivot is $70,333. Resistance is $72,667 (R1), $74,333 (R2) and $76,667 (R3); support is $68,667 (S1), $66,333 (S2) and $64,667 (S3). At $70,500, price is just above the pivot, which many day traders read as a mild bullish bias for the session.

Which candle to use in crypto

  • Crypto trades 24/7, so most charts close the daily candle at 00:00 UTC. Use that day's high, low and close for the next day's pivots.
  • Swing traders use the weekly or monthly candle instead for wider levels that last longer.
  • Treat each level as a zone. Combine it with Fibonacci retracement levels or old support for confirmation.
  • Put your stop beyond the next level and check the trade with the risk reward ratio calculator, then size it with the position size calculator.

FAQ

How do you calculate a pivot point?

Add the previous period's high, low and close and divide by 3. That is the classic pivot. Support and resistance levels are then derived from the pivot and the high-low range.

What is the difference between classic, Fibonacci, Woodie and Camarilla pivots?

Classic uses (H+L+C)/3 with floor-trader formulas. Fibonacci adds 38.2%, 61.8% and 100% of the range to the pivot. Woodie gives the close double weight. Camarilla places tighter levels around the close, which suits intraday range trading.

What time do crypto pivot points reset?

Most exchanges and TradingView close the daily crypto candle at 00:00 UTC, so daily pivots usually reset then. Weekly pivots reset at the start of the week, Monday 00:00 UTC.

Are pivot points reliable?

Pivot levels are widely watched, so price often reacts near them, but they fail regularly. Use them as zones with a stop, not as guaranteed turning points.
