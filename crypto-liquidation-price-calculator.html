Crypto Liquidation Price Calculator

Find the price where a leveraged crypto position gets liquidated. Enter long or short, your entry price, leverage and the exchange's maintenance margin rate. You get the liquidation price, how far price can move against you before it hits, and where your stop should sit so you exit on your terms first. It works for isolated-margin perpetual futures on Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana or any coin, and runs in your browser.



How the liquidation price is calculated

With isolated margin, your margin is 1 / leverage of the position. You are liquidated when losses eat the margin down to the maintenance margin. For a long: liquidation price = entry x (1 - 1/leverage + maintenance rate). For a short: liquidation price = entry x (1 + 1/leverage - maintenance rate). Exchanges also subtract fees and use the mark price rather than the last trade, so treat the result as a close estimate and check your exchange's own number.

Worked example

A 10x long on Bitcoin at $65,000 with a 0.5% maintenance margin liquidates at about $65,000 x (1 - 0.10 + 0.005) = $58,825, a drop of about 9.5%. At 25x the same trade liquidates near $62,725, only a 3.5% move away. Bitcoin moves 3-4% in a day often, which is why high leverage wipes out so many accounts.

How to avoid getting liquidated

  • Set a stop loss well before the liquidation price, and size the trade from that stop with the position size calculator instead of picking leverage first.
  • Lower leverage moves the liquidation price further away. Leverage only changes how much margin you lock up; your dollar risk comes from size and stop distance.
  • Cross margin can use your whole balance to keep a position open, which delays liquidation but can lose more than one trade's margin.
  • Funding payments and fees slowly reduce your margin on long-held perpetual positions.

FAQ

How do you calculate liquidation price for a long position?

Multiply the entry price by (1 minus 1 divided by leverage plus the maintenance margin rate). A 10x long at $100 with 0.5% maintenance margin liquidates near $90.50.

Does higher leverage mean a closer liquidation price?

Yes. At 10x a move of roughly 9.5% against you liquidates the position; at 50x it is under 2%.

Why is my exchange's liquidation price different?

Exchanges use the mark price, tiered maintenance margin rates for bigger positions, and include fees and funding, so their figure can differ slightly from a simple formula.
