What columns to track

Company & job titleBasic record; you will forget which "Coordinator" role was which.

Numbers worth watching

  • Response rate (screens ÷ applications). If it's under about 5%, rework your resume and target roles before sending more.
  • Interview rate (interviews ÷ screens). Low here usually means practicing interview stories.
  • Weekly goal. A steady number each week beats bursts followed by burnout.

A short follow-up email that works

"Hi [Name], I applied for the [Role] position on [date] and wanted to check in. I'm excited about [one specific thing about the team or product], and I think my experience with [relevant skill] would help with [goal from the posting]. Happy to share anything else that's useful. Thanks, [Your name]"

FAQ

How do I track job applications?

Use a spreadsheet with one row per application: company, role, link, date applied, source, contact, status, follow-up date and notes.

When should I follow up on a job application?

About 7 to 10 days after applying, and send a thank-you within 24 hours of an interview.
