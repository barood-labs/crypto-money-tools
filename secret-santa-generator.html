Free Secret Santa Generator (Draw Names Online)

Draw names for your office, family or friends gift exchange in seconds. Enter one name per line, add any pairs who should not draw each other (like couples), set a spending limit, and click draw. Everything happens in your browser: no sign-up, no emails collected, nothing saved.


Tip: let each person click their own name to see who they drew, or the organizer can copy the full list and message each person privately.

Secret Santa rules that keep it fun

  • Set one clear budget. $20 to $30 is the most common range for offices; family exchanges often go higher.
  • Share a wish list. Ask everyone for 3 ideas in the budget, plus a no-go (allergies, no clutter).
  • Pick the date first, and allow at least two weeks for shipping if anyone is remote.
  • Try a theme: under $10, something homemade, a favorite book, or "something cozy".

FAQ

How does a Secret Santa generator work?

It shuffles the names so nobody draws themselves and nobody draws someone on their exclusion list, then shows each person who they are buying for.

How much should you spend on Secret Santa?

Most office exchanges set $20 to $30. Family and friend groups often use $25 to $50. Choose one clear limit so nobody overspends or feels awkward.

Is this Secret Santa generator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up and stores nothing.
