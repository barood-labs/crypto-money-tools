How impermanent loss is calculated

For a classic 50/50 constant-product pool, define the relative price change r = (Anow / Aentry) / (Bnow / Bentry). The LP position is worth 2 × √r / (1 + r) times the HODL value of the same starting tokens, so IL = 2√r / (1 + r) − 1. IL is usually shown as a negative percentage (a loss vs holding). Fee income is estimated as deposit × (APR / 100) × (days / 365) and added back to see whether fees cover the IL over your holding period.

Worked example

Deposit $10,000 into an ETH/USDC 50/50 pool at $2,000 / $1. ETH rises to $3,000 and USDC stays $1, so r = 1.5. IL ≈ −2.02%: the LP position is worth about $12,247 while HODL is worth about $12,500, so you are roughly $253 behind HODL before fees. At a 20% fee APR over 90 days you earn about $493 in fees, which more than offsets that IL in this example. Use the break-even APR output to see the fee rate that would exactly cover IL for your own numbers.

What IL does and does not mean

  • Impermanent means the loss vs HODL shrinks if prices return toward entry; it becomes permanent when you withdraw while prices are still diverged.
  • IL ignores fees, incentives and token emissions. Real LP P&L is IL plus fees (and any rewards) minus gas and any IL from further moves.
  • Stablecoin–stablecoin pools have tiny IL; volatile pairs (ETH/alt, memecoins) can see large IL even when both tokens rise.
  • Staking a single asset has no IL of this kind — compare yield with the crypto staking rewards calculator. Spot P&L after fees belongs on the crypto profit calculator. Custody choices are covered in the crypto wallet guide.

FAQ

What is impermanent loss?

Impermanent loss is the difference between the value of tokens left in an AMM liquidity pool and the value of simply holding those same tokens. It appears when the token price ratio changes; it shrinks if prices return toward the entry ratio and becomes realized when you withdraw.

What is the 50/50 impermanent loss formula?

For a Uniswap V2-style 50/50 pool, IL = 2√r / (1 + r) − 1, where r is the relative price change (A_now/A_entry) / (B_now/B_entry). At r = 1.5 (e.g. ETH +50% vs a stablecoin) IL is about −2.02%.

Do liquidity pool fees offset impermanent loss?

Often partly, sometimes fully. Trading fees (and any incentives) accrue to LPs over time. This calculator estimates fee income from an APR and days-in-pool figure and shows the break-even fee APR that would exactly cover the dollar IL over that period. Real fee APR varies with volume and TVL.
