Trading Risk of Ruin Calculator

Find out if your trading strategy has an edge and how likely it is to blow up your account. How expectancy and risk of ruin work

Expectancy is what you make on average per trade: (win rate x average win) - (loss rate x average loss), in R. Above zero means the strategy has an edge on paper. Profit factor is gross wins divided by gross losses. Risk of ruin is the chance your account falls by your chosen drawdown before the trades run out. Even a winning strategy can be ruined by risking too much per trade, because losing streaks always come. This calculator simulates thousands of random trade sequences with compounding position sizes and counts how many hit the drawdown.

Worked example

A 45% win rate with 2R winners and 1R losers has an expectancy of 0.45 x 2 - 0.55 x 1 = +0.35R per trade. Risking 1% per trade, the chance of a 30% drawdown over 200 trades is low. Risk 5% per trade with the same strategy and the chance jumps sharply, even though the edge didn't change.

How to lower your risk of ruin

  • Cut risk per trade first. Going from 2% to 1% usually lowers risk of ruin more than any change to entries. Size each trade with the position size calculator.
  • Plan for the longest losing streak shown below, because it will happen.
  • Prop firm traders: set "ruin" to your max drawdown as a share of the account and check it with the prop firm drawdown calculator.
  • Use your real numbers from a trading journal, not hoped-for ones. Fifty or more logged trades give a far better estimate.

FAQ

What is a good risk of ruin for trading?

Most traders aim to keep risk of ruin under 1%. If the calculator shows more than a few percent, lower your risk per trade before changing anything else.

How do you calculate trading expectancy?

Multiply your win rate by your average win, then subtract your loss rate times your average loss. A 50% win rate with 1.5R wins and 1R losses has an expectancy of +0.25R per trade.

Can a profitable strategy still blow up an account?

Yes. A positive expectancy only says you win on average. Risking too much per trade lets a normal losing streak cause a large drawdown before the edge plays out.
