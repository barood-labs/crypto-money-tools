FAQ

How much should I save per week to reach my goal?

Subtract what you already have from the goal, then divide by the number of weeks until your deadline. For example, $5,000 over 52 weeks is about $96 a week. Interest from a high-yield savings account lowers this a little.

What is a good savings goal for 2027?

A common order is a $1,000 starter emergency fund, then paying off high-interest debt, then building 3 to 6 months of expenses. After that, set sinking funds for holidays, travel and car costs.

Should I save weekly or per paycheck?

Saving per paycheck is easiest to automate because the money moves before you can spend it. The calculator shows both amounts.
