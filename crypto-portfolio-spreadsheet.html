Build a Crypto Portfolio Spreadsheet

You don't need an app that wants your API keys. A simple sheet with two tabs is enough.

Tab 1: Trades

Columns: Date, Coin, Type (Buy/Sell), Quantity, Price, Fee.

Tab 2: Holdings formulas

  • Bought: =SUMIFS(Trades!D:D,Trades!B:B,A2,Trades!C:C,"Buy")
  • Sold: =SUMIFS(Trades!D:D,Trades!B:B,A2,Trades!C:C,"Sell")
  • Holding: =B2-C2
  • Average buy price: total buy cost ÷ quantity bought
  • Unrealized P/L: holding × (current price − average buy price)

In Google Sheets you can pull prices with =GOOGLEFINANCE("CURRENCY:BTCUSD") (coverage varies by coin).
