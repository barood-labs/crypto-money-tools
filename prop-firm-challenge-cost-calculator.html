Prop Firm Challenge Cost Calculator

What does it really cost to get a payout from a prop firm? Most traders fail the evaluation more than once, so the price on the checkout page is rarely the real cost. Enter your plan's fees and an honest guess at your pass rate to see the expected total spend, the expected number of attempts, and how big your first payout has to be just to break even. It runs in your browser and works for any firm.



How the math works

If each attempt has pass chance p, the expected number of attempts before a pass is 1 / p. Only some funded accounts ever reach a payout, so the expected attempts per payout is 1 / (p x funded payout chance). Multiply by what each attempt costs (fee or reset plus data fees) and add the activation fee for each pass to get the expected cost of one payout.

What the numbers usually look like

  • Industry figures often quoted put evaluation pass rates in the low tens of percent, and the share of funded traders who ever get paid lower still. Use your own track record if you have one.
  • A cheaper eval with a tighter drawdown can cost more overall if it lowers your pass rate.

FAQ

How much does it cost to pass a prop firm challenge?

The sticker price is one evaluation fee, but the expected cost is the fee divided by your pass rate, plus any activation fee. At a 15% pass rate a $150 evaluation costs about $1,000 on average before a pass.

Is a reset cheaper than buying a new evaluation?

Often yes. Many firms sell a reset for less than a new evaluation. Enter the reset price as the cost of each extra attempt to compare.

Are prop firm challenges worth it?

Only if your expected payout is larger than your expected total cost. The calculator shows the break-even payout so you can judge it with your own numbers.
