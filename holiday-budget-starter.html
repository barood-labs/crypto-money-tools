Free Holiday Budget Printable: the Holiday Budget Starter (PDF)

A one-page budget beats a perfect plan you never write down. The Holiday Budget Starter is a free 5-page printable that covers the three things that keep December on budget: a total number, a gift list, and a log of what you actually spend.

Download the free PDF
5 pages, about 13 KB. US Letter, prints fine on A4. No email or sign-up needed.

What's inside

  1. Cover page: undated, so you can reuse it every year.
  2. Holiday Gift Budget: set your total first, then split it by person or group. It includes the costs people forget, like food, decor, cards, postage, wrapping and travel, plus a "save per week" line.
  3. Gift List Tracker: 26 rows for recipient, gift idea, budget, spent, where to buy, bought and wrapped.
  4. Holiday Spending Log: every purchase with a running total, so you know where you stand before you shop again.
  5. Next steps: links to the free calculators below and to the full planner if you want it.

How to use it in 10 minutes

  1. Run the numbers in the free Christmas budget calculator and copy your total onto the budget page.
  2. Split the gift part per person with the gift budget per person calculator.
  3. Going away? Add the trip from the holiday travel cost calculator. Hosting? Use the Thanksgiving dinner cost calculator.
  4. Stick the gift list on the fridge and log every receipt the same day.
