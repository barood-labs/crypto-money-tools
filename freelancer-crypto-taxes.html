Getting Paid in Crypto as a Freelancer

In the US, crypto received for work is generally income at its fair market value on the day you receive it. That value also becomes your cost basis for when you later sell or spend it.

Record two things

  1. Income: the USD value on the day you received it. This feeds into self-employment and income tax.
  2. Later disposal: the gain or loss compared with that basis.

Because nothing is withheld, set money aside every quarter for estimated taxes.
