How to cut holiday travel costs

  • Fly on the holiday itself. Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve afternoon and Christmas Day are often the cheapest days to fly. The Sunday after Thanksgiving is usually the most expensive.
  • Driving wins for groups. Gas is shared across everyone in the car, but plane tickets are not. Three or more travelers going under about 500 miles is usually cheaper by car.
  • Count the "small" costs. Bags, airport parking, rides and pet boarding often add $150 to $300 to a flight.
  • Set up a travel sinking fund. Put the weekly amount from the calculator aside every payday. Use the sinking funds calculator to plan next year's trip too.

FAQ

Is it cheaper to drive or fly for the holidays?

For one or two people going far, flying is often similar in cost and much faster. For three or more people, or trips under about 500 miles, driving is usually cheaper because the gas cost is shared. The calculator compares both for your numbers.

How do I calculate gas cost for a road trip?

Round-trip miles divided by your car’s MPG, times the price per gallon. For example, 700 miles divided by 28 MPG is 25 gallons, and 25 gallons at $3.20 is $80.

How much should I budget for food when traveling?

Many travelers budget $20 to $40 per person per day when staying with family, and more if eating out for every meal.
