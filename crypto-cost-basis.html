How to Calculate Crypto Cost Basis

Cost basis is what you paid for a coin, including fees. When you sell, your gain or loss is proceeds minus cost basis.

Worked FIFO example

DateActionQtyPrice
Jan 2025Buy0.10 BTC$40,000
Jun 2025Buy0.10 BTC$60,000
Mar 2026Sell0.10 BTC$70,000

Under FIFO (first in, first out), the coins sold are the January lot: cost basis $4,000, proceeds $7,000, gain $3,000. They were held more than a year, so the gain is long-term. Under specific identification (if your records support it), selling the June lot gives a $1,000 short-term gain instead.

What to record for every trade

  • Date and time
  • Coin and quantity
  • Price in USD at the time
  • Fees
  • Exchange or wallet

US rules for how brokers report crypto (Form 1099-DA) have been phasing in. Check IRS.gov for the current year and talk to a tax professional.
