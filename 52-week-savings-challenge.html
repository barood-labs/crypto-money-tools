52 Week Savings Challenge Chart: Free Printable

The classic 52-week challenge: save $1 in week 1, $2 in week 2, and so on up to $52 in week 52. That's $1,378 in a year. Use the generator to change the amounts, flip it to reverse order (hardest weeks first, during January motivation), and print your chart.

Tips to actually finish

  • Set up an automatic weekly transfer for the amount on your chart.
  • Keep the chart where you'll see it (fridge, planner, phone wallpaper).
  • Missed a week? Don't quit. Double up next week or pick any uncolored box. Order doesn't matter as long as every box gets filled.
  • Decide what the money is for up front: an emergency fund, Christmas, or a trip.

FAQ

How much do you save with the 52 week challenge?

$1,378 with the classic $1 to $52 version. Doubling every amount gives $2,756.

Can I start the 52 week challenge any time?

Yes. Start in any week and count 52 weeks from there.
