How crypto profit is calculated

Your buy fee comes out first, so coins bought = (investment minus buy fee) / buy price. When you sell, proceeds = coins x sell price minus the sell fee. Profit = proceeds minus investment, and ROI = profit / investment. Because you pay a fee both ways, the price has to rise a little before you are even, which is the break-even price shown above.

Worked example

Invest $1,000 at $60,000 with a 0.6% fee: $994 buys 0.016567 BTC. Sell at $75,000 and the gross value is $1,242.50; after a 0.6% sell fee you keep $1,235.05, a profit of $235.05 (23.5% ROI). Fees cost about $13.46 in total. Compare exchange fees in our crypto exchange fees guide.

Taxes on crypto profit (US)

  • In the US, selling, swapping or spending crypto is a taxable event. Gains on coins held more than a year are long-term (0%, 15% or 20% federal rates); a year or less is short-term and taxed like ordinary income.

FAQ

How do I calculate profit on crypto?

Subtract the buy fee from your investment, divide by the buy price to get your coins, multiply by the sell price and subtract the sell fee. Profit is that amount minus what you invested.

What is the break-even price for a crypto trade?

It is the sell price at which your proceeds after the sell fee equal your original investment. With fees on both sides it is slightly above your buy price.

Do I pay tax on crypto profit?

In the US, yes, when you sell, swap or spend the coin. Long-term gains (held over a year) get lower rates than short-term gains. Losses can offset gains.
